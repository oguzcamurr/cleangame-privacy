Questions, problems or ideas: oguzcamuur@outlook.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I record a ball?

Tap the pins that are still standing, then tap the large button, which tells you what will be recorded (for example "8 · leaves split 7-10"). With no pins selected it records a strike or a spare. Gutter, Foul and Undo are below it. Prefer counts? Choose "Pin counts" from the options button.

How do I fix a mistake?

Tap the frame on the scoresheet and enter its balls again. Only that frame changes; the totals update automatically.

Why are some statistics empty?

Strike, spare and split statistics only use games entered frame by frame. Split and leave statistics need pin entry. Games added with only a final score count toward average, high game and series. Clean Game never estimates missing data.

How do I move my games to a new phone?

With Pro, use More › Back Up Data and save the file to Files or iCloud Drive, then use More › Restore Backup on the new phone. Your device's own iCloud backup also includes Clean Game's data.

I bought Pro but it is locked

Open More › Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account. Pro is a one-time purchase and supports Family Sharing.
