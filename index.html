Clean Game: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026 · App: Clean Game: Bowling Tracker (iOS) · Developer: Oguz Camur

Summary

Clean Game does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking.

Data on your device

Your games, sessions, leagues, bowling balls, notes and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are included in your device's iCloud or computer backups if you have those turned on; those backups are managed by Apple, not by Clean Game.

Exports and backups

If you use CSV export or Back Up Data, Clean Game creates a file and opens the system share sheet. Where that file goes (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, email) is your choice. Clean Game never uploads it anywhere.

Purchases

The optional Clean Game Pro purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. Clean Game only checks with StoreKit whether Pro has been purchased on your Apple Account. It does not receive your payment details.

Children

Clean Game does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
